Ask the graph

Ask about the collection in plain English. An AI model (Claude) turns your question into a SPARQL query, which you can read, and the knowledge graph answers it here in your browser. The numbers come from the graph, not from the model.

Answer

Questions are sent to a small proxy that calls Claude; they are not stored. Each visitor can ask a few questions a minute, within a daily limit for the whole demo. In testing, the model wrote an exactly right query for 19 of 20 questions (evaluation).
