What's in the graph

  • graph/crosswalk: works, merged artists and concepts, with provenance and licences.
  • graph/derived: styles inferred from an artist's movement on Wikidata (mc:inferredStyle), never the museum's claim.
  • graph/alignments: links the project asserts itself, such as landscape (AAT) ≈ landscape (Iconclass).

The museums' full Linked Art records are not loaded here, to keep the page light; each is a static file linked from its artwork page.
